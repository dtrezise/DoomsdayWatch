## Collection route

Use MARAD advisories, dated insurer/broker series, route-specific shipping data and financial disclosures. Avoid unattributed premium anecdotes. Economic advice and trading signals are outside scope.

## Open questions

What observable action occurred, when, and on what authority? Is the activity above a matched baseline? What routine explanation accounts for it? What evidence would change the judgment?
