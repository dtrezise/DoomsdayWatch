# Research program chronology

## 2026-11-01 — Establishment

Preparedness Observatory established an independent public-source research program across eight workstreams: military logistics, emergency procurement, geopolitical developments, civilian infrastructure, medical preparedness, financial and insurance indicators, continuity of government, and astronomical hazards.

The initial research inventory contains 19 leads. Each requires evidence appropriate to its claim, comparison against historical context, consideration of alternatives, and an explicit verification status. Inclusion in the research queue does not establish that a reported event occurred or represents an anomaly.

The founding edition includes a structured event database, citation registry, candidate-anomaly register, 2023–2025 baseline collection plan, methods, and a source-integrity audit. Four bounded findings were selected for public publication. Unresolved claims remain outside the published finding catalog.

## Publication and preservation

The public website provides permanent finding records, source links, confidence and claim boundaries, a timeline, methods, and corrections. Local archival snapshots preserve research versions and original-source captures when available. Weekly investigative maintenance is described in MONITORING.md.

The project does not presume a shared hidden threat. Conclusions must remain proportional to inspected evidence and reproducible comparisons.
