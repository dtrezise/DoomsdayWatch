# Product roadmap

## First release

- GitHub Pages public site, eight workstreams, verified finding library and timeline.
- Permanent finding URLs, share composer, bounded image cards, citation trail and correction history, following the TDS evidence-sharing pattern.
- Local SQLite archive, deduplicated objects, snapshot manifests, portable exports and tested offline restore.
- Recurring investigative maintenance after launch; hypotheses may be supported, expanded, refined or contradicted.

## Future family preparedness planner — explicitly deferred

No form, data collection, purchase recommendations or routing has been implemented in this release.

The intended questionnaire covers household/core family members, trusted contacts, accessibility and medical needs, pets/dependents, available resources, addresses, destinations, meeting points, primary/alternate routes, communications, evacuation decisions, shelter-in-place, and staying safely out of danger. It should distinguish rapid evacuation from longer sheltering and allow household-specific circumstances.

Supply planning: food, water, medicines, energy, lighting, communications, climate-appropriate clothing, transport, fuel, tools and lawful personal safety/defense. Research quantities, storage limits, maintenance cycles, affordability and local requirements from appropriate authorities before giving recommendations. Avoid tactical concealment or combat content; focus on civilian safety and emergency guidance.

Proposed experience: short guided steps, optional answers, explanation of why a question matters, household review checklist, printable contacts/route sheets, offline plan export, and reset/delete. The design should begin with browser-local data, no analytics of answers, no public storage and no collection of family details in the research repository. Explain persistence/loss risks before any save action; any account sync, geocoding, map provider, location sharing or cloud storage needs a separate privacy/security design. The first release does not imply these features are available.

## Later archival infrastructure

Add a physically independent encrypted backup and tested retention strategy before evaluating hosted metadata and object stores. Preserve portable exports so the project is not dependent on one service.
